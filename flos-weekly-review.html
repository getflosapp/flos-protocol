FLOS Field Tool · Weekly Review

The 15-Minute Fitness Command Review.

A practical review template for turning the past week into one clearer decision for the next one.

Do not evaluate the whole plan at once.

A weekly review is not a trial where you pass or fail. It is a short appointment with the evidence. Look for what worked, where friction appeared, and what deserves one deliberate adjustment.

Complete this at the same time each week. Keep the answers honest and specific. You are not trying to write a perfect report—you are trying to make next week easier to operate.

01 / What actually happened?

Record the week as it was, not as you intended it to be.

02 / What worked better than expected?

Name the behavior, condition, or decision worth keeping unchanged.

03 / Where did the system create friction?

Look for the repeated obstacle—not the most dramatic single event.

04 / Score the four pillars

Use a simple 1–10 score based on the week’s evidence.

05 / Which pillar needs attention first?

Choose the lowest or most disruptive pillar. Do not choose all four.

06 / What is the one adjustment for next week?

Make it specific enough to observe. One adjustment is more useful than a complete restart.
