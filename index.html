The scattered-system problem

Your effort may not be the problem.
Your system may be.

You train in one place, track food somewhere else, estimate recovery from memory, and judge progress by how you feel that day.

Then, when results stall, you are left with too many possible explanations:

  • Should you train harder?
  • Eat less or add more cardio?
  • Rest more or change the program?
  • Start over—again?
“When the signals are disconnected, every week feels like starting over.”
The four-pillar framework

Four pillars.
One operating system.

FLOS brings the parts of your fitness process into one reviewable system. Each pillar answers a different question. Together, they show you where your effort is working—and where it is leaking.

01 / FLOW

Energy & readiness

Understand your starting condition before making the day’s decisions. Stop treating every low-energy day as a character flaw.

02 / LOAD

Training & progression

Record what you did, how difficult it felt, and whether your training is moving forward.

03 / OUTPUT

Nutrition & targets

Use practical numbers instead of general intentions, with room for real life and weekly adjustment.

04 / SUSTAIN

Recovery & consistency

Track the conditions that allow your training and nutrition decisions to continue long enough to compound.

FLOS Protocol by Mj Revz

FLOS Protocol was built from a simple observation: many people are willing to work hard, but they do not have a reliable process for understanding what is happening.

Training is recorded separately. Nutrition is estimated. Recovery is remembered only when it becomes a problem. Progress is judged emotionally, and the next adjustment is often based on the loudest opinion available.

FLOS was created to reduce that noise—to help you measure honestly, review consistently, and make one clearer decision at a time.

Questions, answered

Before you begin.

Is FLOS a workout program?

FLOS is broader than a workout program. It is a 12-week self-coaching and review system that connects training, nutrition, energy, and recovery.

Do I need advanced fitness knowledge?

No. You should understand basic wellness and training concepts, but the system is designed to make your process clearer—not to require technical expertise.

Is this medical advice?

No. FLOS is an educational wellness system. It does not diagnose, treat, or prevent medical conditions.

How much time does it take?

The system is designed around short daily observations and a focused weekly review. The purpose is not to create another full-time task.

Which tier should I choose?

Core is the clean starting point. Pro is the complete 12-week system and the best fit for most people who want the full framework. Elite is for expanded customization and advanced resources.

Can I use the free audit without buying FLOS?

Yes. The audit gives you a useful snapshot of your current system. The paid protocol is for people who want the complete structure for applying and reviewing it over 12 weeks.
