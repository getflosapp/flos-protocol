⚡ Morning Flow Protocol

4 steps before the day starts

Takes ~5 minutes · Paper or any notes app works — no tools required
1

Score your wake-up energy from 1–10. Write it down. One number, every morning — that is your baseline.60 seconds · on waking

2

Set one Focus Intent for the day — in 10 words or less. One thing that makes today a win.Write it where you will see it

3

Decide your first meal or calorie target now. Calories first, macros later — remove the noon guessing.Decide once, not all day

4

Decide today: Load day or Rest day? Name it before the day does. Plan beats improvisation.Training scheduled, not hoped for

🎯 Week 1 — Your Only Focus

Keep it boring. Boring works.

Do not add anything else this week
  • Track calories only. Do not obsess over macros yet — one dial before the full dashboard.All week
  • Log one habit per day. Consistency over perfection — a chain you never want to break.AM or PM, your choice
  • Rate every training session's effort from 1–10. Just the number. This is the seed of the RPE system you get in the full card.After each session
  • Do not change your diet drastically. Stabilize the basics first — optimize before you overhaul.Protect the week
